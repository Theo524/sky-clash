# V55.1 — Shinobi Footwork

This release targets Rin Sable's stiffness and gliding after the V55 VRM0 safety repair. Builds directly on V55 installed in the user's GitHub repository. It does **not** add raw Mixamo animations to the unsafe legacy VRM0 clip layer.

## Changes
- Rin's ground walking speed scales separately from Kairo's to allow feet to meaningfully keep up with actual movement; sprint, dash, double jump and flight rules remain separate.
- Rin's idle guard is now asymmetric and open-handed, with one foot advanced, low weight and flexed knees. VRM finger pose no longer forces both hands into a permanent boxing fist.
- A two-link analytic leg solve (0.45 m thigh, 0.40 m shin) drives the support leg, raised swing leg and foot angle. Idle knees stay flexed and the swing knee lifts to recover between steps.
- Ground stride cycles are driven by traveled distance, with 60% stance, 40% swing, smooth ankle pickup, directional strafing and separate backwards travel. Ordinary walk support-foot sweep is scaled to near world movement speed.
- Ninja running retains its swept-back arms and their separate athletic animation; Kairo's original walk, punches, flight and kick remain unchanged.
- Animation damping is faster during ninja walking to prevent high-frequency steps from being visually smoothed into straight legs.
- Added an actual synthetic VRM0 forward-step direction check and a live Fighter update test of Rin walking, strafing, landing and double jumping.
